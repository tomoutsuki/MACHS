About FABEO Attribute-Based Encryption

CP-ABE (Ciphertext-Policy): Data is encrypted with a policy, and users need attributes that satisfy the policy to decrypt.

KP-ABE (Key-Policy): User keys contain a policy, and data is encrypted with attributes that must satisfy the key's policy.

Policy Examples:

  • (role:doctor AND department:cardiology)
  • (clearance:high OR role:admin)
  • role:nurse AND (department:emergency OR department:icu)
